Currently, I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Data Science, Fudan University (2025-Present). I get my Ph.D. degree from Beihang University, advised by Prof. Zhoujun Li and Prof. Dong Xu. I have been selected for several talent programs such as Huawei TopMinds. Moreover, I worked at MSRA (working with Jian Yang, Dongdong Zhang, Furu Wei), Alibaba Qwen (working with Chang Zhou, Junyang Lin), StepFun (working with Duan Nan, Haoyang Huang) and Dolphin AI (co-founder). I maintain stable academic cooperation with LLM teams (e.g. Qwen, Seed, DeepMind, Tencent) in industry.

I am actively looking for research interns and Master/PhD candidates to work on LLM/MLLM research topics. Please feel free to contact me: guohc@fudan.edu.cn
